BUILD THE ARTIST. NOT JUST THE CONTENT.

TreeHouse Creatives helps emerging artists define their sound, image, identity, and the world surrounding their art.

What artist development means here

You already make the art.
We help build the world around it.

Artist development isn't about turning you into somebody else. It's about getting intentional about who you already are — then making sure your music, visuals, story, and presentation speak the same language.

TreeHouse brings those pieces into one creative ecosystem so you don't have to develop every part of your artistry in isolation.

The development process

FROM A SEED
TO A WORLD.

01

SEED

We start with the artist: what you're making, where you're trying to go, what feels clear, and what doesn't yet.

02

ROOTS

We define the foundation — identity, references, sound, visual language, story, and the creative choices that hold everything together.

03

GROWTH

We create. Music gets refined. Visuals get developed. Concepts become tangible assets and a stronger artistic presentation.

04

WORLD

The pieces connect into an artist people can recognize, understand, and remember — without losing what made the art personal.

Meet Tree

I’m Trejohn. I go by Tree, and I make music as TREJXN.

TreeHouse Creatives was built from the belief that artists need space to develop, not pressure to already have everything figured out.

I created TreeHouse as a place where sound, visuals, identity, and creative direction can actually talk to each other. The goal is not to manufacture artists — it’s to help them become more intentional about who they already are.

Through Tree on the Mix, Shot With Tree, and TreeHouse Directions, I help artists shape the world around their work while keeping the artist at the center of every decision.
